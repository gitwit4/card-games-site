Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Play Solitaire Daily ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you visit our website at classicsolitaire.com (the "Site"). Please read this policy carefully.

Information We Collect

We do not ask you to create an account or provide any personal information to use this site. However, like most websites, our server automatically records basic technical information when you visit, including:

  • Your IP address
  • Browser type and version
  • The pages you visit on this site
  • The date and time of your visit
  • The website that referred you here (if any)

This information is used only to keep the site running properly and to understand general traffic patterns. It is not sold or shared with third parties for marketing purposes.

Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by your browser. This site uses cookies in two ways:

  • Advertising cookies — placed by our ad partners (described below) to serve relevant ads.
  • Analytics cookies — used to understand how visitors use the site, such as which pages are visited most often.

You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time. If you disable cookies, ads on this site may be less relevant to your interests, but the games will still work.

Advertising — Google AdSense

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements on this site. Google AdSense uses cookies, including the DoubleClick DART cookie, to serve ads based on your visits to this site and other sites on the internet.

Google's use of the DART cookie allows it to serve ads to you based on your interests. You may opt out of the use of the DART cookie by visiting the Google Ad Settings page.

For more information on how Google collects and uses data, please visit Google's Privacy Policy.

Advertising — Media.net

We also use Media.net to display advertisements. Media.net is operated by Miteno Communication Technology and uses cookies to serve contextually relevant ads based on your browsing activity.

For more information on how Media.net collects and uses data, please visit the Media.net Privacy Policy.

Third-Party Ad Vendors

Third-party advertising companies, including Google and Media.net, may use information about your visits to this and other websites to show you ads for goods and services that may interest you. These companies use cookies or web beacons to collect this information. We do not have access to or control over the cookies used by these companies.

You can learn more about interest-based advertising and opt out through the Network Advertising Initiative (NAI) opt-out page or the Digital Advertising Alliance (DAA) opt-out page.

Links to Other Websites

This site may contain links to other websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites. We encourage you to read the privacy policy of any site you visit.

Children's Privacy

This site is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information to us, please contact us and we will remove it promptly.

California Residents (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to know what personal information we collect, the right to request deletion of your personal information, and the right to opt out of the sale of your personal information. We do not sell personal information. For any privacy-related requests, please use the contact information on our About page.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date at the top. We encourage you to check back periodically.
