About Classic Solitaire

Classic Solitaire is a free website for playing card games in your browser. No download. No app. No sign-up. Just open the page and start playing.

We made this site for people who enjoy classic card games and want a simple, clean place to play them. Everything is easy to read and easy to use — on a phone, tablet, or computer.

Our Games

You can play Baker's Dozen Solitaire, Klondike Solitaire, and FreeCell. All games are free with no limits. More games are planned for the future.

How It Works

The games run right inside your web browser. Nothing gets installed on your device. Your progress is not saved when you close the page — each visit starts a new game.

Advertising

This site is free because of ads. You may see ads while you play. We use Google AdSense and Media.net. These services may use cookies to show ads that match your interests. See our Privacy Policy for more details.
