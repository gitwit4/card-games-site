About Classic Solitaire

Classic Solitaire is a free website where you can play traditional card games right in your browser. No download, no app, no sign-up required — just click and play.

We built this site for people who love classic card games and want a simple, easy-to-use place to play them. Everything is designed to be easy to read and easy to click, especially on a larger screen.

Our Games

We're starting with Baker's Dozen Solitaire and adding more games over time. Klondike and FreeCell are on the way. All games are free to play with no limits.

How It Works

All games run directly in your web browser using standard web technology. Nothing is installed on your computer. Your game progress is not saved between visits — each visit starts a fresh game.
